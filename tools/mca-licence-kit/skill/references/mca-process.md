# MCA process reference (New Zealand)

Source: Misuse of Drugs (Medicinal Cannabis) Regulations 2019 and the MCA guidance
(May 2026 edition), as used in a 2026 amendment. **Check the current forms, guidance and
fee schedule on the Ministry of Health website before each new case.** Values below were
correct in October 2026.

## Which forms

| Route | Forms | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New licence | Form A Section A (applicant, responsible person) + one Section B per location/activity | Statutory declaration before a JP. MoJ criminal-record check on the responsible person: the name must match the ID. |
| Add a location (amendment) | Form LA + Form A Section B for the new location + Section A3 (responsible person declaration) | A location cannot be "moved". Add the new one; remove the old one later on another Form LA. |
| Remove a location / other change | Form LA | Changes to security, SOPs, records, responsible person or activity need prior approval (reg 47). |
| Renewal | Form RA + one Form RB for each location on the licence | RA6 is a statutory declaration. Sign RA3 and swear RA6 in the week of lodging, not months before. |
| New NZ variety | Form D, one per variety | Optional. Only for varieties actually held and recorded, with provenance. |

The MCA invoices. Do not pay before the invoice. The assessment starts after payment.

## Time limits

- The MCA aims to decide within **60 working days** of a complete application. Time spent
  waiting on the applicant does not count.
- Guidance 6.2.1 asks for an amendment **at least 60 working days** before the change takes effect.
  Count working days under the Legislation Act 2019 s 13 (exclude weekends, public holidays
  and 25 Dec to 2 Jan; watch Mondayised holidays).
- **Inspection:** the MCA says when you can ask. Give at least **one month's** notice. The MCA
  inspects only a finished site. Every security item must be installed and tested.
- **Renewal window:** opens about 90 days before expiry and closes about 30 days before expiry.
  Read the exact wording on Form RA and diary both dates on day 1.
- The MCA deals with a **renewal before a concurrent amendment**. An approved amendment keeps the
  licence expiry date, so a new location can need renewing at once. Plan the order: often
  lodge the renewal first, then the amendment, unless the MCA agrees in writing to queue it.

## Lodging

- Email: medicinalcannabis@health.govt.nz. From the applicant's address, cc the consultant.
- One PDF per attachment, named with its number, in the order of the cover-letter list.
  No .docx, no .txt, no photos of forms.
- Videos in separate reply emails (size). Include a shot list.
- Phone the MCA the next working day. Confirm that every file arrived.

## After approval

- Monthly supply returns and the 31 December stocktake continue for every location.
- Transfers between locations go under manifest and are recorded at both.
- Any change to the approved security, SOPs or records needs approval first.

## Other bodies to contact early

- **LINZ:** record of title, deposited plan and easement instruments (who must consent).
- **Council:** zoning, building consent for containers or buildings, signage, discharge to air.
- **beforeUdig / pipeline operator:** before any fence post or earth stake.
- **Police:** tell the local station about the site; record the reference.
- **Companies Office:** directors' full names and industry code match the application.
