# Customer communication

The customer is busy and reads on a phone. Every message has one purpose, a day-by-day
table, references that match the tracker, and links at the end. Write instructions in
ASD-STE100 style: the verb first, one action per line.

## Rules

- One question = one fact. Give an example of a good answer when the fact is hard.
  Bad: "Every entry point on the containers?" Good: "For container C1, list each door in
  order from outside. For example: cargo doors, vestibule, inner door with PIN and fob."
- Ask for a photo when a photo is the evidence (licence, ID, title, certificates, each door).
- Put every question and task in the tracker with an ID, owner, start, due and notes.
- Each working day at 07:30, send a short calendar invite with that day's list.
- Put the reason next to a hard deadline ("consent takes about 20 working days").
- Never tell the customer to change a used record. Tell them to write down the facts.
- Check the customer's email address can open shared Drive files (it needs a Google
  account). If not, share with a second address and say which.

## Templates

### 1. Kick-off (after the intake interview)

Subject: `<Company> - licence application: what happens next`

> Hi <first name>,
>
> Thanks for the interview today. This is the plan.
>
> | Step | Who | By |
> |---|---|---|
> | Check the answers and photos | <consultant> | <date> |
> | Answer the follow-up questions (I send them on <date>) | <customer> | <date> |
> | Start the slow checks: title, owner consent, buyer agreement, council | Both | <date> |
> | Draft package and site plans for your check | <consultant> | <date> |
> | Sign before a JP, then lodge | <customer> | <date> |
>
> Cost to the MCA: <fees> incl GST. Do not pay until the MCA sends the invoice.
> No cannabis may go to the new site until the MCA approves it.
>
> Tracker: <link> · Folder: <link>

### 2. Weekly to-do list

Subject: `<Company> - your to-do list for this week (<d>-<d> <Mon>)`

> Hi <first name>,
>
> The big one this week is <day date>: <milestone>. So <dependency> must be done by <day>.
>
> | Day | What to do | Ref | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Mon <d> | <verb-first task> | XX-15 | <why, contact, phone, hours> |
>
> **Questions for me (reply by <day>)** - one email is fine. Answer each one in full.
>
> | Ref | Question |
> |---|---|
> | XX-01 | <one fact> |
>
> Tracker · Documents to sign · Calendar invites.

### 3. JP signing day

> Take the forms **unsigned**, your driver licence and a pen. Sign **only** in front of the JP.
>
> | Form | You sign | The JP |
> |---|---|---|
> | Form LA | LA4: sign, date, write the place | Witnesses LA4 |
>
> Give the signed forms to <consultant> to scan. Keep the originals.

### 4. Lodge day

> | Job | Who |
> |---|---|
> | Final read of the whole package | <customer> |
> | Sign and date the cover letter | <customer> |
> | Send Email 1 to medicinalcannabis@health.govt.nz from <customer address>, cc <consultant>. Attach exactly the files in the list. Count them. | <customer> |
> | Send Email 2 (videos) as a reply to Email 1 | <customer> |
> | Phone the MCA next working day to confirm receipt | <customer> |

### 5. Third-party chaser (buyer, landlord, installer)

> Hi <name>,
>
> <Company> is applying to the Medicinal Cannabis Agency for <site>. The Agency needs
> <exact document> that names <applicant>, <site> and <your licence number>. Can you send it
> by <date>? A one-page letter of intent is enough for now if the full agreement takes longer.

### 6. Information-request reply

> Tēnā koe <assessor>,
>
> Thank you for your request of <date> (ref <ref>). Our answers are below, in the order of
> your questions. The updated documents are attached as <file numbers>, with changes marked.
>
> 1. <question restated in one line> - <answer, with the evidence file>
>
> Ngā mihi, <responsible person>

Reply within 48 hours. If an answer needs a third party, say when it will come.
