## Stage arc for this install
Selected by the install's stage_arc option. Every dryback band here describes the OVERNIGHT (daily) dryback, from the day's peak to the trough before lights-on. Dryback points are points of TRUE water content from the GrowLabs pages; vendor relative figures are the share of peak. Code converts them to a relative figure from the zone's own peak and sends the zone's observed overnight dryback beside it; neither is a value for dryback_target, which compares other drops (DRY-02). Pore EC is root-zone EC in mS/cm; runoff is % of water fed.
