# Insights and local water-calculation verification

Date: 2026-09-08

## Delivered

`frontend/src/pages/insights.tsx` and its isolated stylesheet replace advanced diagnostic workflows with three native-theme views:

- Root-zone diagnostics: selected-zone VWC/EC values against the actual phase references, explicit unavailable states, current-room notices, combined recorded history and per-zone probe coverage. P2 base thresholds and P3 emergency floors retain their distinct meanings.
- Water and calibration: nominal shot litres, millilitres per plant and seconds from supplied substrate/plant/dripper settings; current configured shot references; a local catch-test calculator converting measured millilitres per dripper and minutes into litres/hour. A measured result can replace flow in the local preview only. No control or configuration writes occur.
- Room map: actual descriptor/setup mappings for pump, mainline, valves, aggregate/raw probes and feed-water sensors. Mappings remain selected-room scoped; missing mappings remain missing. It is explicitly a logical equipment diagram, not a measured floor plan. Aggregate probes invalidated by the adapter are shown as unverified; other states are labeled last reported.

The page calls only the read-only setup endpoint for richer mapping metadata and the existing recorded-history API. Navigation to Setup, Strategy and Sensors stays in the current application.

## Math and limitations

Catch flow is `collected mL / 1000 × 60 / minutes`. Nominal zone shot volume is `substrate litres per plant × plant count × shot percentage / 100`. Nominal duration divides that volume by all drippers’ combined litres/second. Tests confirm that increasing plant count scales volume while duration remains unchanged if emitters per plant remain constant.

Missing or invalid hydraulic values suppress the estimate. Nominal calculations do not include line pressure, valve delays, runoff, runtime caps or controller adjustments, and are labeled accordingly. Recorded mean litres/shot is presented separately; it is not treated as calibration evidence. No yield, potency or environmental-outcome claims were added.
