Athena's grow guide, handbook and educational resources informed this project's crop-steering and irrigation-strategy work. Credit goes to Athena for that source material.

- [Athena Commercial Handbook](https://growershub.athenaag.com/athena-handbook-v41iwrqr): Athena's grow guide and cultivation procedures.

Check the original resource, its edition, measurement definitions and applicability before selecting targets for your own setup.
