## Rulings recorded during review

- **CTL-003:** rescue shots do not bypass an unreadable feed band. Cost if wrong: a rescue can be held during an integration blip.
- **CTL-002:** the ON window counts as the OFF budget; water is counted unless every read is definitely off; after 3 consecutive switch failures a zone is retried at most once per 30 min (persisted, reset on success) with an escalated alert, and rescue obeys the back-off. The pump and main-line read-back's first read comes at the shorter of the lead and 1 s, so normal shots are not delayed. Cost if wrong: a zone with flaky reporting waters at most every 30 min until one success.
- **CTL-030:** equal light hours are invalid (keep last good hours, CS-401), not 24 h light. Real 24/0 support is a feature request.
- **CTL-032:** a shot is refused when the shot-room block cannot be saved; pre-command errors re-raise. An unwritable state file stops shots in every room (fail closed, loud, CS-212). Cost if wrong: one room skips the rest of its zones that pass.
- **CTL-031:** a copied shot waits only after a floor shot, so sibling ramp copies are not halved.
- **INT-002 / INT-005:** recipe services are not hard-deleted (public API, backward-compatible rule); #221 deprecates them with CS-613, removal in a future major release.
- **Issue #195:** P0 is bypassed only at the rescue floor. P0 dryback is already measured from the peak since lights-on.
