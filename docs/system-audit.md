# System audit

Every finding from the five area audits (controller, integration, frontend, website, CI) as one table, ranked by user impact. Audited at 3.17.0 (`81140bd5`). The area maps behind it are summarised in [system-map.md](system-map.md).

## Summary

133 findings: **8 S1** (wrong or unsafe now), **16 S2** (real escape path), **55 S3** (misleading or debt that bites), **54 S4** (tidy-up). Four rows (CTL-030 to CTL-033) were found while reviewing the fixes, not in the original audits.

Plain English: all six controller safety bugs that could water at the wrong time, over-water or silently stop watering (CTL-001 to 005, CTL-032) have a fix PR open, as do the S2 controller and integration rows, except CTL-008 and CTL-031, which are deferred. Nothing below is merged yet, and none of the hardware-touching fixes has been run on real hardware. The two S1 website rows are being fixed in a site PR that is not open yet. Most of the S3 and S4 rows are deliberately left for later.

## Rulings recorded during review

- **CTL-003:** rescue shots do not bypass an unreadable feed band. Cost if wrong: a rescue can be held during an integration blip.
- **CTL-002:** the ON window counts as the OFF budget; water is counted unless every read is definitely off; after 3 consecutive switch failures a zone is retried at most once per 30 min (persisted, reset on success) with an escalated alert, and rescue obeys the back-off. Cost if wrong: a zone with flaky reporting waters at most every 30 min until one success.
- **CTL-030:** equal light hours are invalid (keep last good hours, CS-401), not 24 h light. Real 24/0 support is a feature request.
- **CTL-032:** a shot is refused when the shot-room block cannot be saved; pre-command errors re-raise. Cost if wrong: one room skips the rest of its zones that pass (fail closed, loud).
- **INT-002 / INT-005:** recipe services are not hard-deleted (public API, backward-compatible rule); a later PR deprecates them with a warning.
