Where to put a probe

F1 Zone 3 Back reads 91.4% all day: probably right under a dripper. Move it, and it rejoins the zone value after 30 min of normal readings.
  • Push straight down, prongs fully in, not touching the slab bottom.
  • 5–8 cm from the dripper, towards the middle of the slab.
  • Not at a slab end, a drain slit or a cube edge.
  • Front and back probes: same distance from their drippers.
