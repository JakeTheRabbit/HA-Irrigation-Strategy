# Parity UX spec: beating Growlink and AROYA on their own features

Status: draft for review, 2026-10-22. Input: [parity plan](../plans/2026-10-22-growlink-aroya-parity.md) (G1–G17), Gemini watched-notes #01–19, #21, #24, #25, #77, transcripts #20–#43, and the AROYA irrigation Calendar screenshot. Video numbers are from `playlist.txt`.

Scope follows the plan: no users/roles, tasks, IPM, compliance, climate or lighting. Every interaction calls a `crop_steering.*` service or reads an entity, so AiGrow can drive it without this panel; the page holds no business logic. House rules: each fact once per screen; picture before numbers; problems on the thing that has them, never in a separate list; plain grower words; sections Status / Irrigation / History / Equipment / Settings; room tree is the only room/zone picker on desktop, bottom bar on phone.

---

## 1. Design principles

Each principle starts from something the competitors get wrong, or occasionally right, in the videos.

1. **Saving means it is running, and we prove it.** Growlink has two steps: save, then a separate "Update Controller" push (#04, #13, #56, #65), with a flashing button while a push is pending (#18). The result is a count, "(1 success, 0 errors, 0 warnings)" (#04), or a wait: "Config update is taking longer than usual…" (#13), "Controller update scheduled. Starting..." (#65). *Ours:* one Apply; every changed value is read back and each row shows what the controller now holds.
2. **Changes take effect now, and you see the effect before you commit.** After a Copilot target change, the grower waits "24 hours for your updated suggestions to take effect" (#09). *Ours:* the review step projects the effect ("first shot moves 20 min earlier") on the shared timeline. The engine acts on its next loop.
3. **Edit where you are looking, not in a pop-up three menus deep.** Growlink edits in modals: profile (#03), timers (#04), triggers (#05, #14, #56), offsets (#13), alerts (#21), recipes (#65). A timer is Rules › Folder › Rule Group › Timers › Add › pop-up › Update Controller (#04); a sensor offset is nine steps via a three-dot menu (#13). Even tank overfill protection is a nine-step trigger the grower builds by hand (#56). *Ours:* inline editors or a side sheet that keeps the chart visible; anything showing a problem is at most two taps from its fix; safety guards ship built in.
4. **Undo beats "Are you sure?"** Growlink deletes a program behind "Are you sure… This change cannot be undone" (#06). *Ours:* act immediately, show a 10-second Undo toast, and keep a permanent restore in History › Changes (G8). Confirmation is kept only for things that move water or chemicals, and there it takes the form of hold-to-run.
5. **Problems sit where they happen.** Growlink shows bare counters: "Active alerts: 0" (#02, #07), "Alerts 0" badges (#16) and an Inbox badge (#17). The AROYA Calendar puts "2 Schedules with errors" in a banner, apart from the blocks. *Ours:* the failed shot block is red with a ✕, its room row is tinted and the zone in the room tree carries a flag. Any count only jumps you to those places. It is never a list of its own.
6. **Put facts in fields, not in names.** Growlink's pump setup tells the installer to type the model and dial position into the display name ("P1 at 6", #43). Its substrate equation picker silently does nothing for non-TDR probes (#18), and the recipe form shows "Target pH unavailable" without showing how to enable it (#65). *Ours:* model, dial and solution are structured fields; a control that does not apply is disabled, says why and links to the fix.
7. **Floor tasks are phone tasks.** pH probe calibration happens at the sample spigot with valves closed (#40). Pump setup is done on a phone, through Settings › Configure modules › Digital outputs 16+4 › Device 1 (#43). The Portal is desktop-shaped, with top tabs, a left sidebar, a filter bar and right drawers (#16). *Ours:* wizards are built for one hand, big targets and a screen that stays on (Wake Lock).
8. **Live, with no Apply-to-look or reload.** Growlink charts need an "Apply" button before they redraw (#16), and config pushes end in "Configuration update completed. Reloading..." (#15). *Ours:* filters act instantly, state streams over HA's websocket, and every view is URL-addressable.
9. **Keep what they get right: the plain-sentence rule plus a visual, then go further.** Growlink's rule summary sentence and "Rule Visualized" bar (#05, #12, #14, #21) are good. *Ours:* the same sentence and visual, plus a replay of the rule against the last 24 h of your own data.
10. **Alerts that actually arrive.** Growlink's narrator admits SMS can be deferred "by up to 24 hours" and suggests adding email as a backup (#21). *Ours:* HA companion push with the existing per-phone routing, and a Test button that reports delivery per phone.

## 2. Backlog items

*Where* gives section › tab and URL hash; italic quotes are exact copy; "Review & apply", "Undo", "ShotTimeline" and "pick mode" are the shared patterns in §3.

### G1 Pump capacity and zones-at-once guard

**Competitor:** Growlink only gives a visual hint. The valve chart helps you avoid "triggering more volume … than your pump can handle" (#06). Its "Valve (Exclusive)" type is just a type label (#07). Nothing stops an overlap.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Equipment › Setup › *Pumps and water* (`#equipment/setup?step=pumps`).
- **Pump fields:** each pump gets *Pump output (L/h)* and *Zones at once*. Each zone gets a *Shares a line with* chip group (the exclusive group).
- **Capacity bar:** a horizontal stacked bar shows each zone's dripper flow as a segment against the pump line. It recalculates as you type.
  - Over capacity, the overflow segments turn hatched orange and the sentence reads *"Zones 1–6 together need 1,440 L/h. The pump gives 1,200 L/h. Shots will take turns."*
  - If *Zones at once* is set higher than the pump can carry, Apply stays disabled with *"The pump can't feed 6 zones at once. Set Zones at once to 5 or less."* plus a one-tap **Use 5** fix.
- **At runtime:** a queued shot shows on its zone slab (*"Waiting for the pump — 2nd in line"*, hourglass). ShotTimeline gets a *Pump* lane (area chart of `pump_load_pct` against a 100% line), and a queued block is joined from requested to actual start.
- **States:** empty *"Add your pump's output to stop zones starving each other."*; read-back failure per field; stale shows last values with their age. Phone: bar above fields, chips wrap. The bar is `role="img"` labelled with the sentence; hatch + ▲, never colour alone.

**Platform:** `:has()` flags the pump card when any segment is over (`.pump:has([data-over])`) with no JS state; container queries collapse the legend on narrow cards.

**Contract:**
- `crop_steering.setup_save` with keys `pumps[].max_flow_lph`, `pumps[].max_concurrent_zones`, `zones[].exclusive_group`. The server rejects with code `pump_over_capacity` and suggests `max_concurrent_zones`.
- `sensor.crop_steering_<room>_pump_load_pct`, `binary_sensor.crop_steering_<zone>_shot_queued` (attrs `position`, `requested_at`), CS alert "Shot delayed: pump capacity".

### G2 Feed pH/EC calibration that is actually applied

**Competitor:** Calibration happens at the sample spigot after closing valves and draining (#40). No software step-through is shown. #46 and #47 are title only.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Equipment › Tank and feed, feed probe card: *"Calibrated 23 days ago"* (orange at the due limit) + **Calibrate**. The due flag also sits on the feed pH/EC value on Status.
- **Wizard** (full-screen on phone, side sheet on desktop):
  1. **Prepare:** *"Close the feed line valves and drain the probe loop through the sample tap. Rinse the probe in clean water."* (Per-site wording; not every rig has a spigot.)
  2. **Buffer 7.00** (pH) / **1.413 mS** (EC): big live reading inside a stability ring; *"Moving 0.03 in 30 s"* becomes **Steady** below 0.02 pH (0.01 mS) per 30 s. **Capture** enables only when steady; *Capture anyway* sits in a secondary menu with *"Reading still moving — calibration may be off."*
  3. **Buffer 4.00** / **2.77 mS**, same ring.
  4. **Result:** *"Probe reads 0.12 pH low. Response 97% — healthy."* Under 90%: *"Response 86% — clean or replace the probe soon."* Slope/offset in a disclosure.
  5. **Apply** with read-back; proof row: *"Now reads 4.01 in pH 4.00 buffer ✓"*.
- **States:** probe offline → *"No reading from the feed pH probe for 2 min. Check it's plugged in."*, Capture disabled; leaving mid-wizard keeps the session 30 min (*"Resume calibration"*). Revert: Undo toast, then *Put back previous calibration* in History › Changes.
- **Accessibility:** the reading is `aria-live="polite"`, throttled to one announcement per 5 s plus "Steady"; focus moves to each step heading.

**Platform:** `<dialog>.showModal()` (focus trap, inert background for free); Screen Wake Lock while waiting on a buffer (released on close, skipped if unsupported); live reading via the existing `subscribe_entities` websocket; the ring is a `conic-gradient` on a custom property, animated only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

**Contract:**
- Services: `crop_steering.calibration_start(probe, kind: ph|ec, points)` → `session_id`, `calibration_capture(session_id, point)` → `{raw, stable, drift}`, `calibration_commit(session_id)`, `calibration_revert(probe)`. These extend the existing `calibration_get`/`calibration_record`.
- Entities: `sensor.<room>_feed_ph_corrected`, `sensor.<room>_feed_ec_corrected` (raw entities untouched; attrs `slope_pct`, `offset`, `calibrated_at`), `binary_sensor.<room>_feed_ph_calibration_due`.

### G3 Guided pump prime and calibration

**Competitor:** pumps are set up deep in mobile settings (#43); picking the H.E. Anderson head and dial "will automatically calculate a max gallon per hour", but the installer also types the model and dial into the name ("P1 at 6") to record what the pump is. Priming and calibrating (#44, #45) are title only.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Equipment › Dosing, each pump card (`#equipment/dosing?pump=<id>`). Face: *"Balance · pH up · P1 dial 6 · 300 ml/min (measured 4 days ago)"*.
- **Edit inline:** *Pump* (A3, P1, P2, H4, Peristaltic, Other), *Dial* (only when the model has one), *Solution* (Nutrient, pH up, pH down, Additive). Nominal flow fills itself: *"About 280 ml/min at dial 6"*.
- **Prime:** **Hold to prime** runs only while held, capped at 30 s, with a countdown ring, under a live interlock checklist (*Mix pump off ✓ · Room dosing paused ✓ · Tank not filling ✓*); any ✗ disables it and names the blocker.
- **Calibrate:** *"Put the suction line in a measuring jug with at least 250 ml."* → **Run 30 s** → *"How much did it pull?"* (`inputmode="decimal"`, ml) → *"300 ml/min — 7% above the catalogue figure."* → Review & apply.
- **Wear check:** a calibration-history sparkline: *"Down 12% since March — tubing may be wearing."* Phone: hold button ≥ 64 px; sparkline hidden under 360 px. Keyboard: hold Space; screen readers get **Prime for 5 s**.

**Platform:** Pointer Events with `setPointerCapture` so a sliding finger still counts; `pointerup`/`pointercancel` call stop, and the server enforces the 30 s cap regardless. `navigator.vibrate` ticks at start/stop on Android.

**Contract:** `crop_steering.pump_prime(pump, seconds)` (interlocked server-side, returns blocker codes), `pump_stop(pump)`, `pump_calibrate(pump, seconds)`, `pump_calibration_commit(pump, ml_measured)`; `sensor.<pump>_calibrated_ml_min`, `sensor.<pump>_last_calibrated`; catalogue via `dosing_get`.

### G4 Measured flow: did the water arrive?

**Competitor:** NDK has a flow meter that reports to the controller (#35, #40), but the videos show no commanded-vs-metered reconciliation.

**Our UX.**
- **Mapping:** the flow meter is mapped in Equipment › Tank and feed, with a live **Check meter** test. It runs a 20 s test shot on a chosen zone and shows litres counted in real time: *"Counted 0.42 L — expected 0.40 L ✓"*.
- **Status › Room, zone slab:** the last-shot dot is hollow under 80% (*"Last shot: 1.9 of 2.4 L arrived (79%)"*) and the zone shows *"Less water than expected — check for a blocked line or dripper."* Over 120%, or flow with no valve open, shows on the room header because no zone owns it: *"Water flowing with no zone on — check for a stuck valve or leak."*
- **History › Water use** gains a *Metered* series beside *Estimated*, the only chart of it. No meter: *"Add a flow meter to prove water reached the plants."*

**Platform:** nothing special; live websocket counts drive the Check meter readout.

**Contract:** `sensor.<zone>_flow_ratio` (attrs `commanded_l`, `metered_l`, `shot_id`), CS alerts `blocked_line`, `unexpected_flow`, plus a `setup_save` key `flow_meters[]`.

### G5 Watering timeline (the AROYA calendar, done better)

**Competitor:** the AROYA Calendar (screenshot) has room rows with expandable zone ranges, a lights-on band, grey/blue/red/orange blocks, a now line, 12/24/48 h zoom, filters, search and a "2 Schedules with errors" banner. Growlink's valve chart adds hover detail (#06). Both are desktop grids, and neither distinguishes a sensor-driven future shot from a fixed timer.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Irrigation › **Watering** (new tab, between Current day and Calendar). URL: `#plan/watering?date=2026-10-22&span=24&rooms=F1,F2&q=z3`.
- **Layout:** a ShotTimeline (§3) with a toolbar:
  - date strip (± 7 days, today marked)
  - span segmented control **12 h · 24 h · 48 h**
  - stage chips (Veg, Flower, Mothers)
  - search field (*"Find a zone or valve"*)
- **Rows:** on desktop the room tree stays the room picker. Selecting a room in the tree scrolls to and expands that row. *All rooms* shows every row.
- **Blocks** use the ShotTimeline glyphs (§3). The key difference from AROYA: a sensor-driven future shot is an *expected* dashed outline (*"Expected around 10:40 if drying continues"*), never drawn as certain like a fixed timer.
- **Block details:** a popover with *time · litres (commanded / metered) · phase · why it fired · source (Engine, Timer, Manual, Rescue)*. Future timer blocks offer *Skip this shot*; failed blocks offer *See what happened* (History › Timeline at that minute).
- **Errors:** no banner. A toolbar chip *"2 shots failed ›"* cycles focus through the failed blocks; failed blocks also tick the time axis red and tint their room label.
- **States:**

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Loading | Row skeletons at the last-used row count, with no layout jump. |
| Empty day | *"No watering planned for this day."* plus *Set up a timer* (G7) for timer-mode zones only. |
| Error | *"Couldn't load the watering plan. Showing what we had at 10:42."* plus **Try again**. |
| Stale | Future blocks hatched grey, with *"Live updates paused — reconnecting"*. |
| Demo | Demo badge. Writes stay in the tab. |

- **Mobile:** sticky label column; the time track scrolls with `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` on hour marks, opens centred on now, default 12 h (24 h in landscape); the date strip is a snap-scrolling chip row; rooms collapse to one summary row, tap to expand zones.
- **Accessibility:** each row is a labelled list; each block is a `<button>` with a sentence (*"F2 zone 3, 10:40, 2.4 litres, done."*); roving tabindex (←/→ blocks, ↑/↓ rows, Home = now).

**Platform:** Popover API + CSS anchor positioning for details (Radix fallback where `CSS.supports('anchor-name: --a')` is false, i.e. older iOS WebKit); `content-visibility: auto` on collapsed rooms; `subgrid` for one shared time axis; View Transitions to morph blocks on 12→24→48 (skipped under reduced motion); URL state shared via Web Share on phone or Clipboard on desktop (*"Link copied"*).

**Contract:** `crop_steering.timeline_get(start, end, rooms[])` returns:
- `rows[]` (room → zones/ranges)
- `events[] {id, zone, start, end, kind: done|timer|expected|failed|warning|queued, source, litres_cmd, litres_metered, reason, cs_code}`
- `bands[] {room, lights_on, lights_off}`
- `pump_load[]`

Also `crop_steering.timer_skip(event_id)`. AiGrow renders the same payload.

### G6 Alert rules you can read and test against yesterday

**Competitor:** Growlink alerts are built in a modal from sensor, comparison, threshold, time of day, minimum duration and emails, with a summary sentence (#21). SMS may be deferred for 24 h (#21). Nothing tells you how noisy the rule will be.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Settings › Notifications › **Your rules** (`#settings/notifications?rule=<id>`). The existing CS-coded alerts stay above as *Built-in alerts*.
- **Editor:** the inline sentence builder with 24 h replay (§3). A firing rule badges the thing it watches (tank, zone, probe), not a list.
- **Controls:** an *On* switch; **Send a test**, reporting per phone (*"Delivered to Ben's phone ✓ · Night phone: not delivered — app signed out."*); delete is immediate with Undo. Source not reporting: *"F2 tank level isn't reporting. This rule can't fire until it does."*
- **Mobile:** each token opens a bottom-sheet picker; replay chart full-width, 160 px tall.

**Platform:** native `<select>` for tokens on phone (OS picker), Popover API listboxes on desktop. Pointer Events let you drag the threshold line on the replay chart, with arrow keys moving it in 0.1/1 steps. Replay recomputes debounced at 250 ms.

**Contract:**
- `crop_steering.alert_rule_set(rule)`, `alert_rule_delete(id)`, `alert_rule_test(id)` → per-target delivery, `alert_rule_preview(rule, hours=24)` → `{fires[], series_ref, min, max}`.
- `binary_sensor.crop_steering_rule_<id>`.
- Sources are limited to irrigation: zone VWC/EC/temp, feed pH/EC, tank level, stock, flow ratio, controller heartbeat.

### G7 Timer programs (for mothers, clones, probe-less zones)

**Competitor:** the Growlink "Device Timer" pop-up has Name, Devices, Time of Day, Sync Time, Start, End, Frequency, Duration and Active (#04). There is no preview of what it will do. Quick-edit sub-timers (One-off, Recycle, Simple) sit in a dropdown under the program list (#06). It is invalid until the user hits "Start time cannot be equal to end time" (#11).

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Irrigation › Current day, per zone: *Watering by* **Sensors · Timer · Off**. *Timer* expands an inline card on that zone, no modal. For several zones, tick them in room-tree pick mode (§3).
- **Card:** a sentence with three shapes: *"Water for [2 min] every [30 min] from [08:00] to [12:00], first shot at [08:00]."* · *"Water once at [14:00] for [3 min]."* · *"Water [6] times during [lights-on], [2 min] each."* The window token (Lights-on / Lights-off / All day / Custom) replaces "Time of Day"; "Sync time" becomes *first shot at*.
- **Live preview:** a one-row ShotTimeline of the next 24 h with ghost blocks (drag window ends to edit) and a consequence line: *"8 shots · 16 min · about 1.9 L per plant. Stops after shot 6: daily limit 1.5 L per plant. 2 shots will wait up to 4 min for the pump."*
- **Validation as you type:** *"End must be after start"* beside the field; the preview goes blank rather than wrong. Save is Review & apply; the real blocks then appear on Irrigation › Watering.
- **Empty:** *"No timer yet. Most mother rooms use 2 min every 2 h during lights-on."* plus **Use that**.

**Platform:** the shared ShotTimeline in preview mode, Pointer Events for edge drag, and `structuredClone` of the program to hold the draft vs saved copy for the review diff.

**Contract:** `crop_steering.timer_program_set(program {id, zones[], kind: every|once|spread, duration_s, interval_s, count, window: all|lights_on|lights_off|custom, start, end, first_at, active})`, `timer_program_delete(id)`, `timer_program_preview(program, hours=24)` → events. `select.<zone>_irrigation_mode` = steering|timer|off. All shots pass the existing guards.

### G8 Change history: who changed what, and why

**Competitor:** Growlink has a per-program clock icon showing user/Autopilot changes and suggestions (#06), and "View History" behind a three-dot menu for sensors (#13, #18). It is per object only.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** History › **Changes** (`#history/changes?room=F2&since=2026-10-15&id=<chg>`), following the room tree.
- **Entries:** a day-grouped feed, one sentence each: *"10:42 · Learner raised zone 3 P2 shot from 2.0 to 2.2% — dryback ran 1.4% past target two days running."* Source chips (You · Learner · AI assistant · AiGrow · Setup) and kind chips (Targets, Plan, Recipe, Setup, Calibration, Alerts). Each entry has **Put back 2.0%**, which runs Review & apply. Empty: *"No changes in this period."*
- **In place:** every setting row gets a clock button whose popover shows that setting's last 5 changes plus *See all changes*. History › Timeline shows changes as diamonds that link to the entry without repeating its text.
- **Accessibility:** an `<ol>` of entries with `<time datetime>`.

**Platform:** `content-visibility: auto` on day groups for long feeds, Intersection Observer to load the next 7 days, and Temporal-ready date maths (§3).

**Contract:** `crop_steering.history_changes(room?, since, until?, kinds?)` → `{id, at, source, actor, target, old, new, reason}`, `crop_steering.history_revert(change_id)`. This supersedes `setpoint_log_get`.

### G9 Probe offset and curve

**Competitor:** Growlink's offset is a slider in a modal reached by nine steps, then "Update Controller" (#13). Its substrate equation applies only to TDR, which you learn from narration (#18).

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Equipment › Probes, each probe row.
- **Match a handheld:** *"Your handheld reads: [52.0] %"* → *"This probe reads 49.0%. Offset +3.0."* → *"Zone 3 moisture moves from 49.0 to 50.4% (other probes unchanged)."* → Review & apply. Fusion absorbs the step, so no step alert fires.
- **Curve:** *Substrate* (Rockwool, Coco/soilless, Custom), disabled for probes that report VWC directly: *"This probe already converts to moisture. A curve only applies to raw readings."* The out-of-range flag links to G14.

**Platform:** `:has()` highlights the zone slab on Status when one of its probes has an unconfirmed offset draft (`BroadcastChannel` keeps the draft in sync if Status is open in another tab).

**Contract:** `setup_save` keys `probes[].offset_{vwc,ec,temp}`, `probes[].curve`. `sensor.<probe>_vwc_corrected`. Changes are logged to G8.

**Competitor:** Copying rule groups and presets (#51, #52) are title only. Ours are browser-local today.

### G13 Sensor onboarding kit

Mostly templates and docs. The only UI is in the Setup wizard mapping step, which gets a scale check in place: *"This reads 1400. That looks like µS/cm. Treat as 1.40 mS/cm?"* with **Yes, convert** / **No, it's right**. Contract: `setup_read` returns `scale_hints[]`.

Help › *Placing probes*, plus an inline `<details>` panel on Equipment › Probes: a cross-section SVG of slab and pot (depth, distance from dripper, angle), one sentence per rule. Every out-of-range probe flag links here (*"Reading looks wrong — check placement"*). No contract.

Settings › Display: **EC: mS/cm · PPM 500 · PPM 700** and **Temperature: °C · °F**. Inputs accept and show the chosen unit; storage stays mS/cm and °C; one formatter owns every unit label, never rounding past source precision. Contract: `display_save` keys `ec_unit`, `temp_unit`.

Backend only. The Room header already shows plan day. It switches to reading `sensor.<room>_grow_day`, `_days_to_harvest` and `_stage` instead of computing them client-side, so the panel, AiGrow and wall cards agree. Copy is unchanged: *"Day 38 of 63 · 25 days to harvest · Flower"*.

### G17 System health

**Competitor:** "Modules online %", "1 of 2 Offline" and offline modules on a separate page (#16, #17). #49 is title only.

**Our UX.**
- **Where:** Equipment › **Health** (`#equipment/health`), reached from the room tree's zone flag and the header connection dot.
- **Content:** a ring (*"96% reporting · controller running · add-on 4.12.0"*), then mapped entities by room and kind, silent first, each *last heard 3 min ago* (orange after 2 loop intervals, red after 20 min). An open CS alert sits on the entity row it concerns.

## 3. Cross-cutting patterns

### Review & apply with read-back

All writes follow one flow, built on the read-back already in `lib/client.ts`.

1. **Draft:** changed fields get a dot and the section header says *Unsaved*. The draft lives in `sessionStorage` (survives a panel reload) and is mirrored across tabs by `BroadcastChannel('crop-steering-drafts')`.
2. **Review:** side sheet (desktop) or bottom `<dialog>` (phone), one sentence per change (*"Zone 3 P2 shot: 2.0 → 2.2%"*) plus the consequence from a `dry_run`/`*_preview` call (*"First shot moves 20 min earlier"*).
3. **Apply:** optimistic rows show *Sending…* with a dashed underline; after the call each entity is read back. Success: *"Controller now holds 2.2% ✓"*, underline solid. Failure: *"Controller still holds 2.0%. Nothing else was changed."* + **Try again**, draft kept.
4. **Record:** logged to G8 as *You*; Undo toast shown.

Apply is never two-stage; there is no "Update Controller" anywhere.

### Undo instead of confirm

Every reversible write shows a 10 s sonner toast, *"Saved. Undo"*; Undo calls `history_revert(change_id)` and is read back like any apply. Deletes (timers, rules, presets) are soft, restorable from Changes indefinitely. Confirmation survives only for physical actions: prime and test-run are hold-to-run, Stop all keeps its existing guard.

### Live updates and staleness

- State streams over HA's websocket (`subscribe_entities`, `lib/live.ts`); nothing polls. Any value older than 2 engine loops shows its age in place. On disconnect the existing banner (*"Connection unavailable. These are the last received readings."*) shows and future-facing visuals hatch grey. The last snapshot goes to IndexedDB so the panel opens on last-known values, not a spinner.
- **No PWA inside HA:** the panel is a single-file build inside HA's frontend, whose service worker owns the scope, and the companion app is already the installable shell. Push goes through HA companion notifications (`notify.mobile_app_*`) and the existing per-phone routing, not Web Push.

### Alert in place

A problem gets up to three marks, all on its owner: the thing itself (block, zone slab, probe row, tank), a tone on its room row / room-tree entry, and a time-axis tick where time matters. Counts ("2 shots failed ›") exist only as jump controls cycling focus through those places.

### ShotTimeline (shared by G5, G7 preview, G1 pump lane)

- **Props:** `rows`, `events`, `bands`, `pumpLoad?`, `span`, `now`, `mode: full|preview`, `onBlock`.
- **Layout:** CSS grid. The label column and the time track use `subgrid` so all rows share one axis. Blocks are absolutely positioned at `left: (t−start)/span`. Lights-on is a background band.
- **Block glyphs:** done ● grey · timer ■ blue · expected ▭ dashed blue · failed ✕ red hatch · warning ! orange · queued ⟶ connector.
- **Modes:** `preview` hides the toolbar and allows only window-edge drag; G1 adds the pump-load lane. Container queries shorten labels to the zone number under 480 px. `prefers-contrast: more` thickens borders and swaps hatches for solid fill + glyph.

### Plain-sentence rule builder (G6; G7 uses the same tokens)

- **Sentence is the form:** *"Tell [Ben's phone, Night phone] when [F2 tank level] stays [below] [30%] for [10 min] during [lights-on]."* Each bracket is a token button; there are no separate labelled fields.
- **Replay:** the last 24 h of the source, with a draggable threshold line, breach spans shaded and ◆ where the rule would have fired after the minimum duration. Result line: *"This rule would have fired 3 times yesterday — 06:12, 11:40, 19:05."* · zero: *"It wouldn't have fired in the last 24 h. Lowest was 41%."* · over 10: *"That's 14 messages a day. Try a longer wait or a lower threshold."* Computed server-side by `alert_rule_preview`, so AiGrow gets the same answer.

### Calibration wizard (G2, G3)

`<dialog>`, one step per screen, *Step 2 of 4*, **Back** always available, and the primary button names the action (*Capture*, *Run 30 s*, *Apply*), never a bare *Next*. Live value + 30 s drift + ring to **Steady**. Wake Lock held; leaving pauses the session server-side, nothing runs unattended. Results in health words (*healthy / clean soon / replace*) before numbers.

### Room tree pick mode

For copy (G10) and multi-zone timers (G7), the room tree on desktop gains checkboxes instead of a second picker appearing in the page. **Done** and **Cancel** sit at the tree's foot, and `inert` is set on the main content until one is pressed.

### Command palette and dates

Ctrl K opens a `<dialog>` searching pages, rooms, zones and actions (*"Calibrate F2 feed pH"*). It is bound only while focus is inside the panel, falls back to `/` if the host HA binds Ctrl K, and every result is a URL hash. All time maths goes through one `lib/time.ts` (epoch ms + `controller.timeZone`, `Intl.DateTimeFormat`), so it can switch to `Temporal` ZonedDateTime (lights-on across DST) once HA's browsers ship it.
