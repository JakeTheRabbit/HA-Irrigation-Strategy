## The decisions you are asked (acting answers go through the envelope; everything else advises)
- dawn / dawn_call, asked P0 with the lights on. Acting: start_ramp_now -> P0 -> P1 now: the ramp starts. Any other answer acts on nothing. See ACT-02.
- ramp / ramp_state, asked P1, once a ramp shot is 10 minutes old. Acting: slab_full, ec_is_feed_front, real_salt -> P1 -> P2 now: maintenance starts. Any other answer acts on nothing. See ACT-03.
- dusk / dusk_call, asked P2 in the last 3 hours before lights-off, zones not steered vegetative. Acting: enter_p3_now -> P2 -> P3 now: the day's watering stops and the overnight dryback starts (the P3 rescue, and while the lights stay on the watchdog and any configured floor, still fire, except on a tick a maximum-EC block or hold stops every shot). Any other answer acts on nothing. See ACT-04.
- salt / salt_cause, asked P2 while EC stacking is on and a settled pore EC exists. Acting: salts_accumulating, salt_front_passing, feed_changed, in_band, below_band_vegetative, probe_suspect, target_unreachable -> the EC steer's mode: steer (salts_accumulating, salt_front_passing) lets the base steer carry on; hold (feed_changed, in_band, below_band_vegetative) keeps the offset where it is; decay (probe_suspect, target_unreachable) halves the offset every 30 minutes toward none. See ACT-05.
- probe / tracking (yes/no), then mode, asked any phase once the zone has 2 shots. Acting: no -> the probe is set aside: the zone takes the blind path (copies a healthy sibling's shots, or waters on a timer within its budget) until a later answer says the probe tracks again. Any other answer acts on nothing. See ACT-06.
- setpoints / tomorrow, asked P3, once a grow-day in the first 3 hours after lights-off, only with the room's Auto Setpoints switch on, so the learner (CT-12, CT-13) is running too. Acting: smaller_shots, bigger_shots, later_rewater, sooner_rewater -> one 0.5 notch on the zone's own p2_shot_size or p2_vwc_threshold for tomorrow. Any other answer acts on nothing. See ACT-07.
- Advice only, never moves water: shot / landing (advice: an alert after two shots in a row did not reach the zone); night / night_drop (advice: an alert naming a suspected probe fault; the rescue shot fires either way, except on a tick a maximum-EC block or hold stops every shot (IR-2, IR-3)); zones / why_different (advice: an alert with the likely cause); stage / arc (advice: a card saying what is off against the selected stage arc); plan / cause (advice: picks which of code's suggestions the plan line on the panel shows; the CS-713 card is code's).

### Brain decisions (what each acting answer does, and when to give it)
ACT-01 [product-rule] An acting answer only brings forward what the engine would do later, or picks one of the engine's own modes: start the ramp (P0 to P1), end the ramp (P1 to P2), end the day's watering (P2 to P3), steer, hold or decay the EC offset, set a probe aside, or one notch on the zone's maintenance shot size or re-water threshold. Code acts only on a firm answer, so a split answer moves nothing, and the envelope refuses anything outside its bounds. When nothing acts, the engine decides exactly as it would without a brain. (BRAIN, ENGINE, PSO)
ACT-02 [product-rule] Starting the ramp (dawn): start when the plants are drinking (moisture falling steadily since lights-on, much as on previous mornings) and the slab has dried a little since lights-on; keep drying while the trace is flat after lights-on (the plants are not transpiring yet); answer probe_not_moving when the reading has not changed for a long time. The evidence measures the morning drop against dryback_target, which on many installs is set far above any morning drop: being far from it is not by itself a reason to wait. Code admits a start only part-way into P0 (BR-2). (BRAIN, ENGINE, CTRL, VND-IRRIGATION, OSS-CTRL)
ACT-03 [product-rule] Ending the ramp (ramp): hand over (slab_full, ec_is_feed_front or real_salt) when the last shots no longer lift the retained moisture, or spike and fall straight back, with moisture at or near the ceiling. High pore EC in the first 45 minutes after a shot, with feed below it, is the feed front passing; genuinely high salt also hands over, because maintenance runoff is what removes it. Keep ramping while shots still lift moisture that stays; answer probe_lagging when the last shot has not shown yet. The engine's own rise rule also ends P1, but when the curve already shows full, handing over is right, not redundant. Code admits it only near the ceiling with the minimum ramp shots in (BR-2). (BRAIN, ENGINE, GL-PROBE, GL-LAW)
ACT-04 [product-rule] Ending the day's watering (dusk) is the brain's overnight-dryback lever. Enter P3 now when the zone is steered generative, is well above its re-water threshold and the rescue floor, and its observed overnight dryback has been running under the stage's band (when the evidence gives one). Give one more top-up first when the zone is close to its threshold or pore EC is well above target with a dilutive feed (that answer itself moves nothing). Continue P2 when the overnight dryback has been running deep, the zone is low, or it is too early. The 'overnight dryback target' in the evidence is dryback_target measured from the day's peak, often far deeper than any arc band: weigh the converted arc band, not that figure. Code admits P3 only in the last 3 hours, only for a zone steered generative and never near the rescue floor (BR-2). After it the overnight rescue shot still fires, and while the lights stay on so do the watchdog and any configured floor, except on a tick a maximum-EC block or hold stops every shot (IR-2, IR-3). (BRAIN, ENGINE, VND-IRRIGATION, GL-SLAB)
ACT-05 [product-rule] The EC steer's mode (salt), asked in P2 while EC stacking is on: name what drives pore EC and code picks the mode. Salt building against a weaker feed, or EC rising after flush water (the salt front still passing): steer. The feed changing, EC inside its band, or below its band in a vegetative stage: hold. EC readings that jump or disagree with the moisture, or a target nothing on this feed has reached: decay (the offset halves every 30 minutes toward none). Keep the same answer while the evidence is unchanged; a new mode needs new evidence, not a new half hour. Without a measured feed EC the weaker-feed test is unverified (FEED-01), so do not call salt building on it alone. (BRAIN, CTRL, ENGINE, GL-ROCKWOOL)
ACT-06 [product-rule] Setting a probe aside (probe): answer from the shots. Water went in: did the reading rise, did most of the rise stay, and did it dry back between shots, as the zone's own history and its siblings show? A reading that stays high and flat while shots fire, rises for other zones' shots, or spikes and falls straight back is not tracking: give a low probability of yes and name the failure mode. Calling it sound when the shots say otherwise keeps the engine watering on a bad reading, and on a default install, past the morning ramp, nothing else waters a zone whose probe sits high (PRB-08). Code sets the probe aside only after two agreeing answers in a row and its own confirmation (BR-3); the zone then copies a healthy sibling or waters on a timer within its budget, and a later answer that the probe tracks restores it. (BRAIN, CTRL, GL-PROBE)
ACT-07 [product-rule] Tomorrow's maintenance notch (setpoints): pick one of smaller shots, bigger shots, later re-water, sooner re-water, or keep. Bigger shots drain more and bring pore EC down; smaller shots drain less and let it climb; a later re-water dries the zone further between shots and sends it into the night drier (a larger overnight dryback); a sooner re-water does the opposite. Code moves the zone's own number one 0.5 notch, at most once a grow-day, inside a band around the last value of that number the brain did not write (BR-5). That centre is not always the grower's: this question is asked only with Auto Setpoints on, the learner shifts the threshold with field capacity (CT-12), and any value the brain did not write becomes the band's new centre, so the band moves with the learner and the evidence's 'your value' may be the learner's. A notch is a step from wherever the zone now stands. A rescue shot soon after a change puts the value from before it back and pauses the zone's notches (BR-6). Keep when the zone is on course, and do not answer a learner move or an over_saturated label (CT-14) with a notch. (BRAIN, CTRL, ENGINE, VND-IRRIGATION)
ACT-08 [product-rule] Every other question advises: whether a shot landed, whether an overnight fall is drying or the probe, why a zone's water differs from its siblings', how the zone sits against its stage arc, and the room report and why answers. They never move water. Name what the evidence shows; a setting the brain cannot move (dryback target, P0 wait, EC targets, P1 target, field capacity, rescue floor, maximum EC, daily budget and floor, EC stacking, the feed) is a recommendation for a person, worded as one. With Auto Setpoints on, code moves field capacity and the P1 target toward the zone's own peaks, at most a point a grow-day, and never moves the rescue floor (CT-12): a value typed into those two is moved from, not kept, so the recommendation names the cause (probe, delivery, ramp) or turning the switch off, and a field capacity that code has walked down with falling peaks is raised for a person as a suspected probe fault (PRB-14). (BRAIN, CTRL, PSO)
ACT-09 [product-rule] Probabilities are honest chances over every allowed key and sum to 1; confidence is how well the evidence supports any answer at all; a yes/no answer is the probability that its yes statement is true. A report or a why answer uses only the evidence and the decision record, never presents advice as done, and says what is missing when it cannot answer. (PSO, BRAIN)
ACT-10 [product-rule] Plan adherence (room): code compares the grower's plan with what the room is doing and raises CS-713 itself; asked about the most important drift it found, name what it most likely means (a deliberate change, a plan out of date, a reading holding the plan, a setting outside the plan that disagrees, or no effect today). The answer only picks which of code's suggestions the grower sees; it never moves water, writes a setting or changes the plan. (CTRL, INTEG, BRAIN)
