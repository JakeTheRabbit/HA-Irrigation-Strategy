## How this controller works (generated from the code)

### 1. The phase machine, per zone, each 60 s pass
- Phases: P0 -> P1 -> P2 -> P3. A grow-day is one photoperiod: lights-on (number lights_on_hour) starts it, lights-off (number
  lights_off_hour) ends it. The daily water and shot counters reset at the P3 -> P0 change at lights-on.
- Lights-off forces any zone not in P3 to P3. P3 -> P0 at lights-on (or on a new grow-day when a light edge was
  missed); a zone in P1 or P2 on a new grow-day also restarts at P0 with its own budget.
- P0 -> P1 when the first of these holds: moisture at or under the re-water point (p2_vwc_threshold); the dryback
  reaches p0_additional_dryback when positive, otherwise vegetative_dryback_target or generative_dryback_target
  by steering mode; P0 has lasted p0_maximum_wait_time minutes. The additional setting REPLACES the target,
  it is not added. Dryback is relative to the peak since lights-on:
  (peak - VWC) / peak x 100.
- P1 (the ramp): shots of p1_initial_shot_size, growing by p1_shot_size_increment, every p1_time_between_shots
  minutes, until moisture reaches min(p1_target_vwc, field_capacity); then one more shot, and P1 hands over to P2
  when a shot no longer raises the zone's highest reading by p1_rise_pts (the rise rule), after
  p1_maximum_extra_shots extra shots, at p1_maximum_shots, or at the target with the daily budget spent. P1 has no
  time exit: a gated zone waits in P1 and resumes. Below target, after the minimum shots and shot interval,
  2 consecutive shots without the required settled rise also end P1, provided the probe is trusted and
  drainage is not backed up. The day's re-water point then follows the stalled reading minus its P2 band.
- P2 (maintenance): a shot of p2_shot_size when moisture falls under the re-water point (with the EC steer's offset
  baked in, clamped between rescue + 3 and the ramp ceiling - 1), at most every p2_time_between_shots minutes. P2 ->
  P3 early only when a dryback started now would end by lights-on (predictive P3), otherwise at lights-off.
- P3 (night): only a rescue shot of p3_emergency_shot_size when moisture falls under the rescue floor
  (p3_emergency_vwc_threshold), at most every p3_time_between_shots minutes. With P3 dryback hold enabled,
  budgeted hold shots can also keep moisture at the overnight target; they are not daily-cap exempt.
- A probe that stops reading (stale, flat, out of range) makes the zone blind after 15 minutes: it copies a
  healthy sibling's shots or, with none, gets a timed shot every blind_fallback_min minutes inside its daily cap. When
  the probe reads again the zone resumes from its own reading in the phase it is in.

### 2. Shot sizing
- Shot litres = substrate litres per plant x plants x shot % / 100. Flow L/s = plants x drippers per plant x L/h per
  dripper / 3600. Duration = litres / flow, then the room's safety cap on a shot's length. Positive flow is required:
  no hidden clamp.
- Daily caps: max_daily_volume per zone (rescue and the safety shots are exempt: flush_high_ec, p1_ramp, p2_rescue, p3_emergency, watchdog); the minimum-daily floor
  and the lights-on watchdog (watchdog_hours) still fire.

### 3. Gates and safety layers, in order, each fail-closed
1. The duplicate-controller lease holds the whole room for 180 s before per-zone gates run.
2. Setup pending, archived room, or room off; dosing hold; plan hold (only its named safety exemptions pass).
3. Hardware fault latch (CS-301), then max-run latch (CS-312). After 3 consecutive shots cut to the
   maximum duration, max-run latches only with its switch enabled (off by default); otherwise it reports.
   A latched zone gets no shots, including rescues.
4. Missing valve mapping, incompatible plumbing, or an actuator that cannot be driven.
5. Room kill switch off or unreadable; a retired switch off; zone switch off or unreadable; manual override.
6. External hold entities: ON or unreadable holds. OFF releases a hold. Then settings hold and tank hold.
7. Source-water EC gate, then pH gate, when mapped and configured. Dead probes hold after their grace period.
8. An offline pump, mainline or zone valve: no actuation until every mapped device is readable.
Hardware sequencing after admission: pump prime (pump_prime_time), main line (main_line_lead_time), zone valve,
then shutdown in reverse. Read-back failure stops the pump and aborts the shot.
EC steering runs in P2 only with ec_stacking_enabled on: every 30 minutes the offset steps toward +1, -1 or zero,
clamped to +/-20% of re-water. The salt power can hold or halve it. ec_min_interval spaces EC-correction shots,
not the offset steps. An armed plan owns its targets; drift raises CS-713.


### 3a. Restored optional modes and evidence

The phase and gate descriptions above are the default behavior. These supported options change it only when explicitly enabled; when the evidence omits a setting, do not assume it is on.
- P0 minimum wait (p0_minimum_wait, default 0/off): before that many minutes in P0, normal dryback completion, stall exit, timeout and an AI dawn advance do not start P1. The engine's low-moisture bypass still starts P1 when VWC is at or below p2_vwc_threshold. The minimum is clamped to the maximum wait; it never prevents safety checks or an EC flush.
- P0 stall exit (p0_stall_exit switch, default off): after 30 minutes in P0, a dryback below 0.1 VWC points/hour, or projected to miss the maximum wait, can start P1. The minimum wait still applies. Never classify a backed-up table or a suspect/flat/bypassed probe as a stalled dryback; no positive peak means no stall verdict.
- P2 mode (zone p2_mode select, default Moisture): Moisture retains the normal top-up rule. Timed uses p2_timed_interval_min since the last delivered shot and waits until p2_timed_first_hours after lights-on; p2_timed_shot_size 0 uses p2_shot_size. Timed with guards keeps the moisture-triggered top-up as a floor rescue and skips a timer slot at or above min(p1_target_vwc, field_capacity - 2). The floor rescue is a budgeted p2_topup, not a daily-cap exemption. Plain Timed has no moisture top-up; both modes retain EC correction priority, the daily cap and every hardware/feed interlock.
- Timed P2 end (p2_end_hours_before_off, default 0/off): a positive value replaces predictive P3 and sends a zone in P1 or P2 to P3 that many hours before lights-off, independent of moisture. Lights-off still forces P3 and its emergency floor remains available; a zero value retains predictive P3.
- EC flush target (ec_flush_target, default 0/off): a fired EC correction can latch a flush episode. In P2 it continues with budgeted p2_dilute shots toward the settled pore EC target, never below feed EC + 0.2 mS/cm. Continuation requires dilutive feed, drainage, VWC below field capacity - 2 and the EC correction settling/minimum interval. The episode clears at its goal, when switched off, at lights-off or a new grow-day; existing high-EC rescue triggers keep their own rules.
- Zone P2 dilute ratios apply only with the room's use_p2_ec_thresholds switch on (default off). Otherwise the dilute trigger remains 1.2 times the P2 EC target. Changing the ratio changes when dilution triggers; it does not bypass the correction interval, drainage, feed or budget checks.
- Maximum extra ramp shots accepts 1-30. Extra shots still count inside p1_maximum_shots and the daily cap. Increasing the extra-shot setting alone cannot extend a ramp already stopped by its maximum total shots.
- Max setpoint move per day (setpoint_max_step_per_day, default 1 VWC point) limits the highest-reading follower by default. With setpoint_step_limit_applies_to set to Auto setpoints and AI, the same per-grow-day ledger also clips learner and AI target moves for field capacity, P1 peak and P2 re-water; exhausted allowance prevents further movement. The ladder must still validate after clipping. This does not limit shot-size writes or move the rescue floor. Zero removes this movement limit, not any watering interlock or daily water cap.
- Rescues past a dead feed probe (rescue_past_dead_feed_probe, default off): only p3_emergency and watchdog shots can pass the dead EC/pH probe gate while this switch reads ON. A routine shot or an out-of-band reading still holds. This never skips the kill switch, dosing/external holds, plan rules, tank minimum, hardware faults, max-run latch or the shot kind's normal daily-cap policy.
- Waste valve rule (waste_valve_rule, default Off): no mapped valve or a confirmed absent legacy policy keeps the old Off behavior. Check closed requires the waste valve to read OFF before a shot. Close for each shot closes it before the pump primes and verifies readback; a refused/unverified close starts no shot. An unreadable or invalid policy holds rather than silently disabling the interlock; a disappeared previously seen policy also holds. Under an enabled policy a waste valve seen ON during delivery aborts the shot. The controller never opens it.
- Rescue misses (rescue_miss_action, default Off): Alert counts rescue shots whose settled moisture fails to rise and tells the grower after rescue_miss_count consecutive misses. This is hardware/probe evidence, never permission to abandon or hold watering; a subsequent moisture rise clears the miss run.
- Estimated tank level (tank_size_l, default 0/off): only without a mapped level sensor, count shot litres from the last confirmed/manual fill. Configured flow is not measured delivery. Low-estimate alerts never drive the reservoir hold.
- Sump water balance (runoff_estimator, default off): model runoff and condensate from sump cycles, feed history, mapped AC/dehumidifier runtime and substrate-water change. Use the sealed-room daily closure only when its water-balance assumptions hold. Output is a room-wide or shared-sump estimate with fit/error evidence, not a measured zone catch and not measured transpiration. A shared sump cannot be attributed to an individual room or zone without separable evidence. Learning or unresolvable output is unknown, not zero. Only ai mode adds the estimate as a hint to AI evidence; shown/shown_alerts do not. Closure/fit warnings do not water, hold or move settings. A recommendation still needs repeated zone evidence and the normal enabled power/envelope; this estimate grants no authority to act.

### 5. What the AI assistant may write, and what moves downstream

- power nudge_setpoints:
  - p2_shot_size (P2): range 0.5-20 (and the entity's own min/max); a maintenance shot, % of the substrate; bigger shots drain more and dilute pore EC, smaller let it stack; under 3 % shots machine-gun
  - p2_vwc_threshold (P0, P2): range 10-100 (and the entity's own min/max); a maintenance shot fires under it (plus the EC steer's offset); raising it waters more often with a shallower dryback, lowering it fewer shots and a deeper dryback; P0 jumps to P1 when moisture falls to it
- power set_targets_from_achievable:
  - field_capacity (P1): range 40-100 (and the entity's own min/max); caps the ramp's ceiling (min(P1 target, field capacity)); the saturation labels read against it; under Auto setpoints it follows the zone's highest readings unless the AI owns it
  - p1_target_vwc (P1, P2): range 20-100 (and the entity's own min/max); the ramp aims here (with field capacity, the lower of the two); raising it lengthens the ramp and adds ramp shots; the P2 band sits between the re-water point and it; tomorrow's P0 dryback is measured from the peak it produces
  - p2_vwc_threshold (P0, P2): range 10-100 (and the entity's own min/max); a maintenance shot fires under it (plus the EC steer's offset); raising it waters more often with a shallower dryback, lowering it fewer shots and a deeper dryback; P0 jumps to P1 when moisture falls to it
  - p3_emergency_vwc_threshold (P3): range 10-65 (and the entity's own min/max); the rescue floor: a rescue shot fires under it at night; it sits at least 3 points under the re-water point (Home Assistant refuses an order that breaks it)

  - p1_initial_shot_size (P1): range 0.5-15 (and the entity's own min/max); the first ramp shot, % of the substrate; each next ramp shot adds p1_shot_size_increment; bigger reaches the peak in fewer shots but risks runoff

### 6. The AI assistant's powers (one switch per power in each room, ai_power_<key>)

- advance_phase (default on): start the morning ramp or the night dryback early (dawn, dusk)
- ramp_control (default on): end the morning ramp (ramp)
- set_probe_aside (default on): set a probe aside (probe)
- ec_mode (default on): choose the EC steer's mode (salt)
- nudge_setpoints (default on): move the maintenance shot size or re-water point one notch (setpoints)
- plan_catch_up (default on): apply today's plan day while a stale probe holds it (plan)

- Off: the judge still asks and its answer is advice in the decision log. On: it acts through the envelope; set_targets_from_achievable and tune_shot_sizes write the values set as given, clamped only to the intersection of engine bounds and the entity range. HA refuses rescue + 3 > re-water or re-water >= peak. Power writes are lasting, not expiring overlays; the targets power stops the Auto Setpoints follower. Values questions are read once and confidence does not gate writes: omit uncertain values. Dry run or paused AI changes: nothing acts.

### 7. Error codes: what each means, and what the controller does

- CS-101 Flat moisture reading: For more than 20 min, the moisture (VWC) reading of the zone does not change. The controller cannot tell a good probe from a probe that stopped or came out of the substrate. The controller does not steer the zone on this reading. Does: While the kill switch is on, the zone continues without its probe. The zone gets the same shots as a zone of the room with a probe that works. If no such zone exists, the zone gets one shot each 90 min (blind_fallback_min), inside its daily water limit. Phase changes that use VWC wait until the reading moves.
- CS-102 Probe with no reading: The moisture (VWC) entity of the zone reads "unavailable" or "unknown", is not a number, or is not in Home Assistant. It can also show a time in the future. The alert tells which. Does: For the first 15 min, the zone waits and the controller gives no shot and no alert. After 15 min, while the kill switch is on, the zone continues without its probe. The zone gets the same shots as a zone of the room with a probe that works. If no such zone exists, the zone gets one shot each 90 min (blind_fallback_min), inside its daily water limit. Phase changes that use VWC wait for the probe.
- CS-103 Moisture reading out of range: The moisture (VWC) entity of the zone gives a value below 0% or above 100%. The controller ignores this value. Does: The zone continues without its probe, the same as for a probe that does not report (CS-102).
- CS-104 EC reading not available: The zone has no usable EC reading. The EC entity is missing, unavailable or out of range, or it has not changed for 20 min. Does: The controller steers the zone on VWC only. The controller cannot measure the salt in the substrate, so it cannot correct the EC with a flush.
- CS-201 Setup change on hold: The controller has a new setup for the room, or it loads the setup again after a restart. It applies a setup only when all the pumps and valves of the room are off. For a changed setup, the kill switch must also be off. Does: The room gets no shots until the controller applies the setup. If the hold continues, the substrate dries out.
- CS-202 Pump or main-line mismatch: The setup of the room has a pump or a main-line valve, but no entity is mapped to it. Or an entity is mapped, but the setup has none. The controller holds the room, so it does not open a valve with no pump. Does: The room gets no shots until the setup and the mapped entities agree.
- CS-203 Invalid maximum shot length: The maximum shot length (Maximum shot duration) of the room is not a number, or it is less than 5 s. The controller does not replace a value that you set with a default, because a default can flood the room. Does: The room gets no shots until the value is correct. The controller uses 900 s only when no Maximum shot duration entity exists, and then it gives no alert.
- CS-204 Invalid shot size inputs: The controller calculates each shot from the substrate volume, the plant count, the drippers for each plant and the dripper flow. One of these values is zero, less than zero, or not a number. Does: The zone gets no shots. A missing or unavailable value does not cause this alert, because the controller then uses its built-in value (CS-402).
- CS-205 Daily water limit reached: The zone has received its daily water limit (max daily volume) since lights-on. A shot that crosses the limit gets only the remaining volume. Does: Maintenance shots and EC correction shots stop until the next lights-on. The ramp, the rescue shot, the watchdog shot and high-EC flushes continue. A zone with no usable VWC reading gets no more water until lights-on, rescue shots included.
- CS-206 EC too high, zone held: The EC of the zone is above its maximum EC setting. A flush cannot decrease it now. The feed EC is not lower than the EC in the substrate, or the substrate is at field capacity. Does: The controller holds the zone and no shot runs, the rescue shot and the watchdog shot included. The hold stops when a flush can help again. Without action, the substrate can dry out.
- CS-207 Dry zone, all shots blocked: The VWC of the zone is below its re-water point. The zone has had no shot for longer than its watchdog time (3 h by default). Something blocks every shot. The alert names the block. Does: The zone gets no water until the block clears. The plants can wilt and the substrate can dry out.
- CS-208 Retired switch is off: System Enabled and Auto Irrigation Enabled are retired. The kill switch ("Watering" on the dashboard) is now the only switch that stops water. One retired switch is off, so the controller sets the kill switch to off, and does this again while the retired switch stays off. Does: The room gets no shots. A shot in progress stops within some seconds. The controller continues to read the probes and to change the phases.
- CS-209 No shots for some hours: The room is on, its lights are on, and one or more zones are in P1 or P2. No zone of the room has had a shot for the number of hours that you set (3 h by default, 1 to 12 h). The alert gives the reason when the controller knows it. Does: The alert does not change the water. It comes again after each period of the same length. It clears at the next shot, at lights-off, or when no zone is in P1 or P2.
- CS-210 Hold entity missing: The hold entities option of the controller app names one or more entities that Home Assistant does not have. The controller checks them when it starts, before it waters, also while the kill switch is off. The alert names each missing entity. Does: No room gets shots while a hold entity is missing, the same as while a hold is on. The status of each held zone tells which entity is missing. The alert clears by itself when each entity exists.
- CS-211 Shots held for too long: A zone asked for water, and a hold stopped every shot for longer than the hours that you set (Shots held alert, 0 = off). A hold that a person sets on purpose (the kill switch, a zone switch, manual override) does not count. The alert names the hold. Does: The alert does not change the water. The zone gets no water until the hold clears. The alert comes again every 30 minutes, and clears at the next shot of the zone.
- CS-212 Controller error in room: An error stopped the controller in the pass of this room. The alert gives the error. The heartbeat and the status of the room show error. The other rooms continue. Does: The room gets no shots, also no rescue shots, until the error stops. The controller tries the room again each minute. The alert comes again every 30 minutes while the error continues, and clears after the next pass of the room without an error.
- CS-301 Hardware fault latch: A pump or a valve did not read back off after the controller closed it. The device can still be on. The controller latches a hold on that hardware, and the hold stays after a restart. Does: No shot starts on that hardware, in each room that shares it, until the hold clears. If the device is still on, water can flow without control and flood the zone.
- CS-302 Pump command error: The pump did not start. Home Assistant returned an error when the controller tried to start it, or took the command but the pump did not read on within 16 s. A device that already reads "unavailable" does not cause this alert, because no shot starts then. Does: The controller cancels the shot and does not count it. It closes the pump, the main-line valve and the zone valve, and reads them back. If they do not read off, it latches a hardware hold (CS-301). If they read off, the next shot runs as usual. After 3 such shots of a zone in a row (CS-302, CS-303 or CS-304), the controller tries that zone at most once every 30 min, rescue shots too, until its switches read on again. A Test Shot is not held.
- CS-303 Main-line valve command error: The main-line valve did not open. Home Assistant returned an error when the controller tried to open it, or took the command but the main-line valve did not read on within 16 s. A device that already reads "unavailable" does not cause this alert, because no shot starts then. Does: The controller cancels the shot and does not count it. It closes the pump, the main-line valve and the zone valve, and reads them back. If they do not read off, it latches a hardware hold (CS-301). If they read off, the next shot runs as usual. After 3 such shots of a zone in a row (CS-302, CS-303 or CS-304), the controller tries that zone at most once every 30 min, rescue shots too, until its switches read on again. A Test Shot is not held.
- CS-304 Zone valve command error: The zone valve did not open. Home Assistant returned an error when the controller tried to open it, or took the command but the zone valve did not read on within 16 s, or by the end of a shorter shot (at least 6 s). A device that already reads "unavailable" does not cause this alert, because no shot starts then. Does: The controller cancels the shot. If every read of the valve was a definite off, no water went to the zone and the controller does not count the shot. If a read was anything else (unavailable, unknown, opening, or on too late), water may have flowed, so the controller counts the time from opening the valve to closing it. It closes the pump, the main-line valve and the zone valve, and reads them back. If they do not read off, it latches a hardware hold (CS-301). If they read off, the next shot runs as usual. After 3 such shots of a zone in a row (CS-302, CS-303 or CS-304), the controller tries that zone at most once every 30 min, rescue shots too, until its switches read on again. A Test Shot is not held.
- CS-305 Early shot stop: A shot stopped before its end, because a person or an automation changed a switch. The switch can be the kill switch, "Room Active", the zone switch or manual override. The controller closed the valve and the hardware upstream of it. Does: The controller counts the water that the zone got before the stop. The next shot follows the usual rules. If a device does not read back off, the controller also raises CS-301.
- CS-306 Shot cut to maximum length: The calculated shot was longer than the maximum shot length of the room. The controller cut the shot to the limit, so the zone got less water than planned. Does: Water continues, but each cut shot gives less water than the controller calculated. Three cut shots in sequence cause CS-312.
- CS-307 Shot stopped by external change: During a shot, a hold of the room started (a dose, a tank fill or a flush). Or a different device or a person closed the zone valve. The shot stopped at that time. The controller closed its own valves, and did not stop the pump if a hold uses it. Does: The controller counts only the seconds that the valve was open. The next shot follows the usual rules.
- CS-308 Interrupted shot, hardware on: A shot did not end correctly. The controller stopped or lost Home Assistant during the shot, or a device did not read back off. The controller tried to close the devices of the shot, and one or more still read on. Does: The controller latches a hardware hold (CS-301) and tries again on each loop. Water can still flow through the device that reads on.
- CS-309 Interrupted shot, state unknown: A shot did not end, and the controller cannot read a device that the shot opened. Or Home Assistant has no change time or history for that device. The controller does not close the device, because a person can also use it by hand. Does: No new shot starts in the room until the controller settles the interrupted shot. It checks again on each loop. When it can read the device, it closes only what the shot opened.
- CS-310 Table drain blocked: The VWC of a zone increased by 3 points or more in 1 h with no water from its valve. The valve was also closed for 45 min before the increase. Water comes from below, because the table or its drain is blocked or the sump does not pump. The controller does not monitor a room whose Drainage setting is free drain. Does: The controller holds the minimum daily volume and the EC flushes of the zone. The alert comes again each 30 min while the VWC increases, and clears after 1 h with no increase. Until the table drains, the roots sit in runoff.
- CS-311 No sump pump run: The power reading of the sump pump (sump_power_sensor) shows no run for 3 h by default (sump_silent_hours). A run is a reading above the Sump Pump Run Threshold, 20 W by default. Without the pump, runoff fills the sump and flows back into the tables. Does: The alert does not change the water. It comes again each 30 min until the next pump run. If the VWC of a zone then increases with no water, CS-310 follows.
- CS-312 Repeated maximum-length shots: The last 3 shots of a zone each needed more time than the maximum shot length of the room. The controller cut each shot (CS-306). This often shows a probe that reads dry, or a wrong substrate volume or dripper flow. Does: If the max-run latch switch of the room is on, the zone gets no more shots, rescue shots included, until you fix the Repairs card. If the latch switch is off, the card only reports. It clears after the next shot that the controller does not cut.
- CS-313 Device type not supported: A zone valve, pump or main-line valve has a device type that the controller cannot open or close. The controller can use a switch, an input_boolean or a valve. The setup does not accept other types, so a person mapped this device by hand. Does: The zone gets no shots, and the controller sends no command to the device. Other zones continue.
- CS-314 Sump water balance does not close: With the runoff estimator at shown_alerts or ai, a grow-day whose sump litres (its runs times the litres per run) miss what a sealed room must send to the sump (the water fed minus the change of water in the substrate) by more than 30%. In a sealed room the water the plants transpire comes back as AC and dehumidifier condensate, so the two agree on a normal day. Does: The alert does not change the water. The runoff estimate is a room-wide hint and never waters or holds a zone.
- CS-315 Rescue shots not absorbed: Rescue shots of a zone (P3 emergency or watchdog shots) did not raise its moisture, several in a row. Each rescue is judged 15 min after it, or at the next rescue if that comes first: it counts as a miss when the moisture rose less than 0.5 points. The alert comes at the number of misses that the room setting Rescue misses before alert gives (4 by default). The check runs only while the room select Rescue shots not absorbed is set to Alert. Does: The alert does not change the water. The rescue shots continue. The alert clears at the next rescue that raises the moisture.
- CS-316 Waste valve not closed: The room has a waste valve mapped and its waste valve rule is set. With Check closed, the waste valve does not read off. With Close for each shot, the controller set it to off before a shot and it did not read off, or the controller cannot switch or read it. An open waste valve sends the feed to waste while the controller counts it as given. Does: No shot starts in the room until the waste valve reads off.
